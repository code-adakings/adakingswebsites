When we opened our first Adakings location, we set out with one goal: serve food good enough to make people proud to call it Ghanaian. We never put a number on what success would look like — we just kept showing up, kept cooking, and let the community decide.

That community has now helped us sell over 200,000 meals.

## Built one plate at a time

Every one of those meals represents a customer who chose us — for a lunch break, a family dinner, a celebration, or just because they were craving something good. That trust is not something we take lightly.

## What's next

This milestone isn't a finish line. It's fuel. We're opening new branches, expanding our catering program, and inviting entrepreneurs across Ghana to bring Adakings to their own communities through franchising.

Thank you for 200,000 meals. Here's to the next 200,000 — and beyond.
